Privacy Policy

Inkly — Your Calligraphy Companion · by Inkaura

Last updated: September 2026

Inkly is built by Claudia Luque, trading as Inkaura. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Inkly app.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Inkly, we collect the following:

  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Practice session data — lessons completed, streaks, and weekly progress you record in the app.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app, such as which features you use and when.
  • Device identifiers — anonymous identifiers used to maintain your session.

We do not collect your name, payment information, location, contacts, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Inkly account.
  • To save and display your calligraphy practice progress.
  • To send account-related emails (e.g. password reset, email verification).
  • To improve the app based on how it is used.
  • To generate AI-powered writing feedback using the Claude API by Anthropic.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We apply row-level security to ensure your data is only accessible to you.

4. AI Features

Inkly uses the Claude API by Anthropic to provide writing suggestions and feedback. When you use AI-powered features, relevant content from your session may be sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. This data is not stored by Anthropic beyond the scope of the request. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with the service providers necessary to operate Inkly:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Anthropic — AI writing feedback (Claude API)
  • Resend — transactional emails
  • Expo / EAS — app distribution

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

Inkly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Inkly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
